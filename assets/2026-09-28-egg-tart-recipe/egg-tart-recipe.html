The crust

Make the custard first as this uses some of the eggs.

Ingredients

  • 190g (1½ cups) plain flour + 50g (⅓ cup) corn flour
  • 110g (1 stick) unsalted butter, room temp
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp beaten egg from custard recipe

Method

  1. Sift the plain & corn flour, sugar, salt, and the softened butter into a large bowl. Knead it altogether until you're happy with the consistency.
  2. Add the beaten eggs (for the coloring of the crust) and keep kneading until smooth.
  3. Leave in the fridge for 30 minutes until the dough is slightly firmer.
  4. Lightly oil the tart pans while waiting.
  5. Divide dough into 6 - 8 tart portions.
  6. For each portion; roll into a ball, place it in a tart shell and press it into the pan with your fingers. Keep it an even thickness and avoid a thick bottom.

The custard

Ingredients

  • 4 beaten eggs (reserve 2 tbsp for the crust)
  • 175g (¾ cup) hot water
  • 5 tbsp sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • 60g (¼ cup) milk

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Heat up milk on a pot or pan right until before boiling, and then let it cool to room temp.
  3. Dissolve the sugar and salt in the hot water, then let it cool to room temp also.
  4. Mix the sugar water, evaporated milk, and beaten eggs altogether until combined.
  5. Strain out any lumps, then chill while you make the crust.
  6. After the tart shells are added with the dough, fill the shells with the mixture till 80% full. Bake for 15-20 minutes, until the surface turns golden.
  7. Cool for a few minutes, then go crazy.
